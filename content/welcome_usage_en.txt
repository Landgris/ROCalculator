Notes:
* M.DEF/M.RES calculation is included; skills can be set to ignore RES — please report any errors
* Elemental affinity currently matches the live game version (same as KRO); you can switch to the older table
* It's recommended to first calculate your current gear and confirm the damage is correct before starting to plan new gear
* Emblems (enemy side) now boost damage on all skills — please be careful when using them
* When calculating skill damage while dual-wielding, physical damage does NOT include the off-hand weapon's ATK/refine, but magic damage does
  Normal attack damage while dual-wielding is not calculated
* Normal attack crit: +100% damage, Skill crit: +50% damage
* HP/SP recovery formulas only support level 240/250/260 Dragon Peer/Royal/Sacred Judge
* 4th job: stat points extended to level 275, job bonus applies at level 60
